# The Wizard

## *The Runaway Apprentice*

> **Read aloud:** You left. Your master, your tower, your books — whatever it was, you walked away and ended up here, at the edge of the world, where nobody asks where you studied. Magic is rare on the frontier. People need you. That's new.

## Spells

You know *Read Magic* (always prepared) and one spell you memorize each morning. Pick one:

| Spell | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| ☐ **Sleep** | 2d8 HD of creatures in a 40' area fall asleep. No save. Your best spell. |
| ☐ **Charm Person** | One humanoid treats you as a trusted friend. Save negates. Lasts until they save again. |
| ☐ **Magic Missile** | 1d6+1 damage, auto-hit, 150' range. Reliable. Boring. Keeps you alive. |
| ☐ **Shield** | AC 2 [17] vs. missiles, AC 4 [15] vs. melee. Lasts until end of combat. |
| ☐ **Light** | 15' radius on an object or a creature's eyes (blinding, save negates). 12 turns. |

You cast your prepared spell **once**. Then it's gone until you sleep 8 hours and study your spellbook.

## Choose One: Why Did You Leave?

This is the secret you're keeping. It determines what you believe about power.

☐ **Your master was doing something wrong.** Experiments, summonings, something that crossed a line. You couldn't stop it, so you fled.

| | |
|---|---|
| *Reputation* | Principled. The Minister trusts you. The Captain thinks you're soft. |
| *You start knowing* | The name of an ancient site nearby that your master was researching — and one reason it should stay sealed. |

☐ **You wanted power your master wouldn't teach.** They were too cautious. Too slow. You came to the frontier because the old places have magic nobody controls.

| | |
|---|---|
| *Reputation* | Ambitious. The Captain sees you as useful. The Minister watches you carefully. |
| *You start knowing* | The name of an ancient site nearby — and one rumor about what power might be found there. |

☐ **You were sent here and you don't know why.** Your master said "go to the lake outpost" and nothing else. That was six months ago. No letters. No explanation.

| | |
|---|---|
| *Reputation* | Outsider. Nobody fully trusts you yet. But nobody's asked you to leave, either. |
| *You start knowing* | A phrase your master made you memorize. You don't know what it means. The Minister's hymnal has the same phrase in it. |

## Choose One: What Did You Take?

When you left, you grabbed one thing. It's in your pack right now.

☐ **A scroll you weren't supposed to read.** One extra 1st-level spell — roll randomly or the DM picks. You can copy it into your spellbook given time and materials.

☐ **A strange component.** An amber stone, a vial of silver liquid, a feather that doesn't fall. The DM will tell you what it's for when the time comes.

☐ **Nothing.** You left clean. But your master's voice is still in your head, and sometimes when you cast a spell, you hear it.

## Fate Ability: "I've Read About This"

**Uses:** 1/session

When the party encounters something ancient, magical, or monstrous, declare one true fact about its nature, origin, or behavior. You are adding lore to the world. The DM builds on it.

> *Example:* "These symbols on the wall — they're a ward. The old empire used them to bind things in, not keep things out. Whatever's behind this door, they wanted it to stay." The DM decides what it is, but your fact stands.

| Level | Escalation |
|-------|------------|
| 2 | 2 uses/session |
| 3 | Declarations work on anything — politics, geography, history. You read a lot. Cross-specialty declarations cost 1 FP. |

---

## Fate Points

You start with 3. They refill after a full rest.

| Cost | Effect |
|------|--------|
| 1 FP | Reroll one attack, damage, or save |
| 1 FP | +4 to a save (after seeing the roll) |
| 2 FP | Cheat death — drop to 1 HP instead of dying |

The DM awards 1 FP for doing something bold, clever, or true to your character.
