# The Elven Thief

## *The One Who Opens*

*Year Two. Make your choices in the Fort Cards intro; this playbook tells you what they do.* The intro is [`intro/index.html`](../../intro/index.html). If you do not have it, tick the ☐ boxes here and fill in the Fort Card at the end by hand.

> **Read aloud:** You came to the Fort the way water comes into a boat. Nobody saw the hole. Then you were simply there, dry-footed, asking what was for supper. Last year you went into the Gorgon Caves behind the others and came out ahead of them, with your hands full and your face empty. In the Year Between you held the Pen for one summer, and you wrote the Fort's longest days in a hand so neat that Wick said you must have forged it. Your people count years the way the Fort counts bushels. You count locks. Now the milestone on the Old Road has begun to hum, and the old places are waking, and every one of them has a door. A door, you have always held, is only a question asked politely.

## Thief Skills

You do not cast spells. You have hands, patience, and a roll of tools in oilcloth.

| Skill | L1 | **L2** | L3 | L4 | How it works |
|-------|----|--------|----|----|--------------|
| **Climb Sheer Surfaces** (CS) | 87% | **88%** | 89% | 90% | Roll once for each 100'. Fail: you fall from halfway. |
| **Find or Remove Treasure Traps** (TR) | 10% | **15%** | 20% | 25% | Small traps on chests and locks. One roll to find, one roll to remove. Traps in rooms: search like anyone else. |
| **Hear Noise** (HN) | 2-in-6 | **2-in-6** | 3-in-6 | 3-in-6 | Roll d6. Needs quiet. See the elf note below. |
| **Hide in Shadows** (HS) | 10% | **15%** | 20% | 25% | The DM rolls in secret. You always think it worked. You cannot move or attack while hidden. |
| **Move Silently** (MS) | 20% | **25%** | 30% | 35% | The DM rolls in secret. You always think it worked. *Quiet Feet* adds 20%. |
| **Open Locks** (OL) | 15% | **20%** | 25% | 30% | Needs your tools. One try per lock. Fail, and that lock beats you until you gain a level. |
| **Pick Pockets** (PP) | 20% | **25%** | 30% | 35% | −5% for each level of the victim above 5th. Roll more than twice your number: the victim notices. |

**Elf and thief hearing do not add.** An elf hears at doors on 2-in-6. A thief hears anywhere quiet on 2-in-6, then 3-in-6 from level 3. Use the better number: 2-in-6 at levels 1–2, 3-in-6 at levels 3–4.

**Backstab.** Attack an enemy who does not know you are there, from behind. Roll d20 + 4. Hit: roll damage twice and add. The DM decides if the enemy is unaware. Hide or Move Silently first.

**Read Languages.** From level 4, you read ordinary writing in any language on 80%. Not magic.

Your skills are low at level 2. One in four, one in five. Do not trust them alone. Make a plan that still works if the roll fails.

## Choose One: What Did You Take from the Gorgon Caves? (Year One)

The others came out with a story. You came out with something in your pocket.

☐ **A ward-eye.** A glass eye the size of a plum, prised from the queen's wards. It went cold when the wards went dark. Some nights it is warm again. The DM will tell you what it is for when the time comes.

☐ **A key that fits nothing.** Bronze, as long as your finger, from a niche nobody else saw. You have tried it in every lock at the Fort. You have not tried it in every lock in the world.

☐ **Nothing.** You came out with empty hands, and everyone believed you. Except Wick. Wick counts everything. He has not said what he counted.

## Choose One: Why Do You Stay at the Fort?

A thief can leave any place by morning. You have not. Why?

☐ **Wick counted you.** When Wick wrote the list of the Fort's folk, he wrote your name on it. Nobody has listed you as belonging anywhere before.

☐ **Maren gave you a key.** In the Year Between, the Captain gave you the key to the storehouse. She did not ask you to promise anything. That is why you have kept it. She retires soon. You want to give it back to her yourself.

☐ **There is a city under the lake.** People say Halcyon lies drowned under the Deep, and that its lamps still burn. Cities have doors. You have never met a door you could leave alone.

## Fate Ability: "I Was Already Here"

**Uses:** 2/session at level 2 (1/session at level 1)

**Lane:** places. A room, a road, a building, a cave: its shape, its ways in and out, who has passed through it.

Before the DM describes a place, declare one true fact about it, as if you scouted it earlier. You were here last month. Or last year. Or you came in through the window while everyone else was at the gate. The DM builds on your fact.

> *Example:* "The back door of Mile-House 13 sticks in wet weather, so the agents always use the front. Nobody has checked the cellar hatch since spring."

Say it **before** the DM describes the place, not after. Once the DM has described it, your fact can only add, not undo.

| Level | Escalation |
|-------|------------|
| 2 | 2 uses/session. |
| 3 | You can also say you left something here: one ordinary item from your pack (a rope, a spike, a candle stub), where you need it now. Declarations outside your lane cost 1 FP. |
| 4 | 3 uses/session. |

## Fate Points

You have 3. They refill at each Fort Turn and at the start of each session.

| Cost | Effect |
|------|--------|
| 1 FP | Reroll one attack, damage, or save |
| 1 FP | +4 to a save (after seeing the roll) |
| 1 FP | State one more true fact in your lane, after your free uses |
| 1 FP | **Year Two:** spend it when you use a spell or a power with a limit (once per room, once a day). You write part of its effect. |
| 2 FP | Cheat death — drop to 1 HP instead of dying |

**Writing part of the effect.** The power works as written. Then you say one more thing it does, within its nature: what it shows, what else it touches, one small extra result. The numbers stay. The DM tells you if you reach too far.

> *Example:* With *Locksmith's Eye*, you ask "Is this chest trapped?" and spend 1 FP. The DM answers truly. You add: "and its key hangs on a nail in the next room." It does.

Your limited power is *Locksmith's Eye*, if you chose it. Without it, spend your points on the other rows.

**Bonds.** Your Fort Card names one bond. If you and another PC name each other, you each get 1 extra FP each session. You can spend it only to help that partner.

The DM awards 1 FP for doing something quiet, patient, or generous with what you took.

## Combat Reference

| | |
|---|---|
| **Movement** | 30' a round in leather, and attack; or run 90' (no attack) |
| **Melee attack** | d20 + 0 ≥ target's ascending AC = hit. Short sword 1d6. |
| **Missile attack** | d20 + 2 ≥ target's ascending AC. Shortbow 1d6, thrown dagger 1d4. |
| **Backstab** | d20 + 4. Double damage. Unaware enemy, from behind. |
| **Hiding** | Hide in Shadows: no moving, no attacking while hidden. |
| **At 0 HP** | Dead (unless you spend 2 FP) |

> **Your Role:** Scout and opener. You go first, quietly, and come back to say what is there. You find the trap on the chest and the second way out. In a fight, you are not a front-liner (d4, leather). Shoot, or wait for the opening, then backstab once. Your best tool is not a skill roll. It is *I Was Already Here*, used before the DM describes the room.

> **For 5e players**
> - **AC 14** is your 5e AC. **HP** mean the same thing.
> - **Attack:** THAC0 19 [+0] means +0. Roll d20 + 0 (+2 with the bow or a thrown dagger) against AC. There is no proficiency bonus.
> - **Saves:** roll d20 and meet or beat the number. Lower is better. Death ≈ CON save, Wands ≈ DEX, Paralysis/Petrify ≈ CON, Breath ≈ DEX, Spells ≈ WIS.
> - **Thief skills** are percentages, and low at first. Read Move Silently and Hide in Shadows as Stealth, and Open Locks as thieves' tools, at about −2 against DC 15. Climb is Athletics you almost never fail. Hear Noise is a Perception check that succeeds 2 times in 6.
> - **Backstab** ≈ Sneak Attack: +4 to hit and double damage, only on an unaware target from behind.
> - **Elf:** darkvision 60'. Ghoul paralysis cannot touch you.
> - **Advantage** does not exist here. Spend a Fate point, or make a good plan.
