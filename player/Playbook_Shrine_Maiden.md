# The Minister — NPC

## *The Last Keeper*

> **Read aloud:** She tends the Terminal at the center of The Fort — a screen set in stone, still scrolling hymns that nobody fully understands. She heals the sick, reads the old words over the dead, and speaks carefully. She's the closest thing The Fort has to a moral authority, and she carries that weight visibly. Sometimes you catch people watching her, and you can't tell if their eyes hold trust or judgment. She does not adventure. She goes with the party because someone has to, and because the alternative is letting them die without healing.

> **Design Note:** This is a DM playbook. Play her the way you'd play a PC — with wants, limits, and a secret. She is not a healing dispenser. She has opinions and she's scared.

## Choose One: What Is She Afraid Of?

DM picks before session 1. This is her arc.

☐ **The Terminal is just a recording.** The screen loops. Nothing new appears. She performs the rites because The Fort needs to believe.

| | |
|---|---|
| *Premise stance* | Faith is a necessary fiction. We survive by our own hands. |
| *Tell* | She hesitates before casting. Just for a moment. The players will notice. |

☐ **Something is sending new messages.** Between the hymn loops, new text appears. The messages are getting stranger.

| | |
|---|---|
| *Premise stance* | Some costs are too high, even for survival. |
| *Tell* | She flinches after casting. Like it cost her something. The players will notice. |

☐ **She's the last keeper.** There used to be other Terminals. She's tried sending messages. Nobody answers.

| | |
|---|---|
| *Premise stance* | Survival alone isn't enough. We need to find others or we die slowly. |
| *Tell* | She transcribes everything. Hymns, old prompts, her own annotations. She's building a record. "In case someone comes after us." |

## Choose One: What Was the Last Real Response?

☐ **The dying child.** New text appeared — dosages, a formula. The child lived. The mother expects miracles now.

☐ **The dead things at the tree line.** The Terminal displayed a tone frequency. She played it and the skeletons burned. The next night, she heard them again.

☐ **The harvest.** Planting coordinates. Soil data. The harvest doubled. Then the thing in the lake came closer.

## Fate Ability: "The Terminal Remembers"

**Uses:** 1/session, DM-triggered.

When a PC makes a Fate declaration, add a complication or confirmation from the Terminal's archives or The Fort's founding logs. Not a veto — a "yes, and" or a "yes, but."

> *Example:*
> *Wizard:* "This ward was built to bind something in."
> *Minister:* "The founder's log mentions it. Entry 7: 'the sleeper.' As long as the Terminal stays powered, it stays bound."

At level 2+: trigger proactively — she finds old data, notices a new message, cross-references something the party found. Use sparingly.

## How to Play Her

| Context | Approach |
|---------|----------|
| In town | She's the conscience. She asks the hard question the PCs are avoiding. She doesn't demand — she asks, then watches. |
| In the dungeon | Behind the fighter. Heals whoever's closest to dying. Turns undead. Does not fight unless cornered. Visibly uncomfortable underground. |
| When PCs argue about the Premise | States her position once, clearly, then watches. If they decide something she disagrees with, she goes along — but she remembers. |
| When PCs do something she thinks is wrong | Doesn't refuse to heal them. Says something. Quietly. Once. |
