# The Fighter

## *The Next Blacksmith*

> **Read aloud:** Everyone expected you to take over the forge. Your hands know iron — how to shape it, how to mend it, how to break things with it. But when The Fort needed another sword on the wall, you picked one up instead. Now you're something the community didn't plan for, and the forge is cold.

## Fate Ability: "I Know How This Was Made"

**Uses:** 1/session

When you examine a crafted object — a lock, a weapon, a wall, a trap mechanism — declare one true fact about how it was constructed and what its weakness is. The DM incorporates this. You're not casting a spell; you just know iron and stone.

> *Example:* "This portcullis — the welds are shoddy. One good hit on the left hinge and it drops." The DM makes it so.

| Level | Escalation |
|-------|------------|
| 2 | 2 uses/session |
| 3 | Declarations work on natural structures (cliffs, cave formations, load-bearing walls). Can spend 1 FP to declare outside your specialty. |

## Fate Points

You start with 3. They refill after a full rest.

| Cost | Effect |
|------|--------|
| 1 FP | Reroll one attack, damage, or save |
| 1 FP | +4 to a save (after seeing the roll) |
| 2 FP | Cheat death — drop to 1 HP instead of dying |

The DM awards 1 FP for doing something bold, clever, or true to your character.
