# Two in Six: notes

The OSR pack of the Stage for the elven thief. It plays her five Fort Cards scenes as a pop-up book that prints every chance before you choose, rolls small honest dice in the open, and always offers a plan that needs no roll. Contract: `stage/SPEC.md`. Guide: `stage/GUIDE.md`. Its sister is the Magic Module, which teaches the table (the DM's jobs); this book teaches the dungeon (the odds).

This file follows `GUIDE.md` section 2: the plan, then the checks, then one SET BRIEF per set.

## 1. Plan

### Character

The elven thief of Year Two, "The One Who Opens" (`player/year-two/Playbook_Elven_Thief.md`). Level 2, Summer Pen holder. She went into the Gorgon Caves behind the others and came out ahead of them, with her hands full and her face empty.

### The new perspective: the odds are on the page

B/X dice are small and honest. The book prints every chance before the choice, rolls it in the open, and always offers a plan that needs no roll. The playbook says it: "Make a plan that still works if the roll fails." Time is a cost: a plan takes turns, and every second turn the book checks for wandering monsters.

The lineage is structure only: the B/X procedures themselves (roll-under X-in-6, the thief's percentages, the ten-minute turn, the wandering check every second turn). No name, text, map or character comes from any TSR or WotC product.

| Odds as printed | Where | What it teaches | Note |
|-----------------|-------|-----------------|------|
| Hear noise 2-in-6 (`1d6` under 2) | `s1` plate; the listen option | Roll-under X-in-6; an elf hears at doors on 2-in-6 | `under6` |
| Search 2-in-6 (`1d6` under 2) | `s1` plate; the search option | An elf finds secret doors by searching on 2-in-6 | `under6` |
| Wandering monsters 1-in-6 (`1d6` under 1) | `s1` the two check groups | A check every second turn; the same die, a smaller number | `wander`, `turn-clock` |

| `s2` the singing Shallows | "A signal. Something under the water keeps time." One note, the same hour each night. | A signal that keeps one hour can be tested. Time it against a bell for a month. | The start of a test. |

## 7. SET BRIEFS

Eight sets. The paper-kit agent builds each from the brief below. Positions are fractions of the book box. Everything the player must read, and every tab, flap, wheel and ticket, sits in the top 40 % of the stage (y no more than 0.38), where a phone shows it. Colours come from theme tokens only. Otus ink is black on cream (`ink`, `paper`, `tint-1`, `rule`); in the Blueline map style the same strokes use `blueline`. Elmore paint uses a warm wash with `plate-gold` and `plate-umber`.

The Lens pull-tab is a `pulltab` layer with `"tally": "lens"` and `"value": "machine"`: the hymn reading is the front layer (`art`) and the machine reading slides out from behind it (`under`). It does not glow, pulse or blink. A tab stub shows at the edge so a reader can find it. A `pulltab` near the right edge (x + w/2 about 0.87) costs the text the least width.

The Referee Note is a `flap` layer with `"opensOn": "reveal"`: `art` is a sealed paper tag reading REFEREE NOTE; `under` is a small ink drawing of the note's subject. Text on a tag is at least 10 px at 360x640.

The volvelle for `1d6` is a `volvelle` with `"range": [1, 6]` and `"center": 1`. Its success faces are shaded: 1 in the heavier tint (every X-in-6 roll in this book hits on a 1) and 2 in a lighter tint (only the 2-in-6 rolls hit on a 2). A `1d100` has no volvelle: the wheel must not turn for a roll whose total is outside its range (check the kit; see section 8).

## 8. Open questions for the lead

1. **Roll labels on `under` rolls.** The roll panel prints "Success" or "Fail" for a non-table roll. For the wandering check, "Success" means something comes by. The `wander` note explains it. If `view.js` honoured `label` on any roll, `{ "success": { "label": "Something passes" }, "fail": { "label": "The dark stays quiet" } }` would fix it. Not done: it is a shell change. Tell me if you would rather the check be a `table` roll (`"1"` and `"2-6"`, with labels).
2. **The volvelle on a `1d100` roll.** The `plate` set holds a `1d6` wheel, and the same set plays the `1d100` lock roll. The brief says the wheel must not turn for a total outside `range`. The paper kit's behaviour for that case is the kit agent's to check.
3. **`sets.json` is missing.** `validate` on the pack fails until the sets agent adds it. The eight set ids are `cover`, `crack`, `plate`, `pen-summer`, `salt`, `muster`, `loft`, `milestone`.
4. **504 routes.** `test.mjs` plays one route per distinct end state. `SAMPLE=<n>` plays fewer.
5. **Search success.** The seam beside the plate is a hook, not a room. If the DM Guide keys the plate, the seam line and the chalk arrow in the ending are easy to drop (`found`).
